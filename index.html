Nothing counterfeit gets through.

Buying pre-owned luxury from a stranger should not be a gamble. Here is exactly what happens between your payment and your parcel.

How authentication works
  1. 01

    Listed

    Sellers upload the exterior, interior, hardware and serial, plus proof of purchase where they have it. Listings missing required angles never reach the feed.

  2. 02

    Sent to us, not to you

    Anything over JOD 350 travels to our Amman authentication centre first, on a prepaid the buyer label. Your payment is held the whole time.

  3. 03

    Verified against reference

    Stitch density, hardware engraving, date codes, lining and leather grain, all checked against house references for that model and production year.

  4. 04

    Released, then paid out

    Verified pieces ship on with a numbered authentication card. The seller is paid once you accept. Anything that fails goes back, and you are refunded in full.
