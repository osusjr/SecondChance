Protected from payment to handover.

Buying pre-owned luxury from a stranger should not be a gamble. Buyer Protection is free — a courtesy from the platform to our members, on every order. Here is exactly what happens between your payment and the handover.

How Buyer Protection works
  1. 01

    Listed

    Sellers upload the exterior, interior, hardware and serial, plus proof of purchase where they have it. Listings missing required angles never reach the feed.

  2. 02

    Your payment is held

    The seller is not paid up front. Buyer Protection holds your payment until the piece is in your hands and you have accepted it.

  3. 03

    Hand over on your terms

    You and the seller agree a time and place that suits you both, anywhere in Jordan. Until the deal is done, the payment stays held — it is only released once the buyer confirms the order.

  4. 04

    Released, then paid out

    Accept the piece and the seller is paid, minus the platform fee. Not as described? Open a return within 3 days of the handover and you are refunded in full.
