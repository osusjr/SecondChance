The Maison

A resale house built on trust.

Every piece authenticated, every piece documented.

RAÉCAE started in Amman with a simple idea: the region deserves a serious place to buy and sell luxury fashion — where authentication is rigorous and every piece's history travels with it.

The House

Run like a fashion house,
not a listings site.

Pieces are curated, authenticated and photographed by us before they are sold, and the collection is deliberately small.

Three things matter here. Trust — nothing is listed unverified. Discovery — browsing the archive should feel like finding something, not scrolling a feed. And the pieces themselves — something with a documented history is worth more and kept longer.

“Every piece leaves here with its history documented.”

Every item is examined by machine and by hand, then issued a digital passport — a record that stays with the piece through every future owner.

Today

A curated luxury resale platform.
