Questions of Trust

Asked, answered.

What happens if the AI is uncertain?
Uncertainty is treated as a routing signal, never a verdict. Any piece scoring below 95 passes to a senior authenticator for physical examination — hand, loupe and reference plates. The house's judgement is final, and nothing is certified on probability alone.
Do you authenticate items bought elsewhere?
Yes — this arrives as a Phase III service. Bring a piece purchased anywhere and the house will examine it, score it and issue its digital passport, whether or not it is ever consigned to the vault.
What does the passport contain?
The passport number; the maison, model and year; materials; the serials and stamps examined; the condition report; the AI confidence score; the expert countersignature; and the estimated resale value at issue. Future chapters — price history and changes of hands — are appended for life.
What about counterfeits?
A counterfeit never enters the vault. It is returned to its sender, or destroyed with their consent — and it is never relisted, under any circumstance. Zero tolerance is a number in our record, not a slogan.
