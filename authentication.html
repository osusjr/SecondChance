Trust and safety

Every piece over JOD 350 is verified before it reaches you.

Buying pre-owned luxury from a stranger should not be a gamble. Items above our threshold pass through our Amman centre and are checked by a specialist in that house before they are released, and your payment is held until they clear.

The process, end to end

  1. 01

    Listed by the seller

    Photos of the exterior, interior, hardware and serial, plus proof of purchase where they have it. Listings missing required angles are held back from the feed.

  2. 02

    Collected and sent to us

    Once sold, anything over JOD 350 goes to our Amman authentication centre on a prepaid the buyer label, never directly to the buyer. Your payment is held throughout.

  3. 03

    Checked against reference

    Authenticators compare construction, hardware, stamps and materials against house references for that specific model and production year.

  4. 04

    Released, then paid out

    Verified pieces come back with a numbered authentication card, ready for the handover you arrange between you. The seller is paid once you accept. Anything that fails is returned and you are refunded in full.

What our authenticators check

  • Stitch count, density and alignment against the model reference
  • Hardware weight, plating, engraving depth and screw type
  • Date codes, serials and heat-stamp typography
  • Lining material, interior stamps and pocket construction
  • Leather grain, edge painting and glazing
  • Dust bag, box, card and receipt consistency

Buyer Protection

Added to every order at 5% of the item price plus 500 fils, shown on the listing before you buy. It covers:

  • Your payment is held. The seller is not paid until the piece is verified and handed over.
  • 14-day returns. Full refund if the item is not as described, not authentic, or never arrives.
  • Handover on your terms. You and the seller meet where it suits you — payment stays held until you accept.
