Buyer Protection

What the fee covers, when your payment is released, and how refunds work.

What it costs

Buyer Protection is added to every order at 5% of the item price plus 500 fils. It is shown on the listing before you buy, so the price you see beside the item and the total you pay are never a surprise at checkout.

What it covers

Three things, on every order, with no separate claim to open first.

  • Your payment is held and the seller is not paid until the piece is verified and handed over
  • A 14-day window to return anything not as described, not authentic, or that never arrived
  • A handover arranged directly between you

When the seller gets paid

Once the piece is with you and you accept it, or automatically once the 14-day window closes without a return. If you open a return, payout is paused until it is resolved.

What it does not cover

Ordinary wear consistent with the stated condition, a change of mind on fit where the measurements were listed accurately, and items collected in person outside the platform. Buying or paying outside SecondChance Collective removes every protection on this page.
